## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Open the Smart Banking application in your browser
2. Click **Register** on the welcome screen
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Full name** — your complete legal name (2–100 characters)
   - **Email address** — must be a valid email you can access; you will receive a verification code here
   - **Phone number** — include country code (e.g. `+237612345678` for Cameroon)
   - **Password** — minimum 8 characters; choose something strong
4. Click **Create Account**
5. Check your email — you will receive a 6-digit verification code within 1 minute

### Verifying Your Email

After registration, you must verify your email before logging in:

1. Open the verification email from `noreply@smartbanking.com`
2. Copy the 6-digit code
3. On the verification screen, enter your email address and the 6-digit code
4. Click **Verify Email**
5. Your account is now active

> **Code expired?** Verification codes expire after 10 minutes. Click **Resend Code** to get a new one.

### Logging In

1. Click **Login** on the welcome screen
2. Enter your registered email address and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. You will be taken to your Dashboard

> **Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot Password** on the login screen, enter your email, and follow the OTP reset instructions sent to your inbox.

## Your Wallet

### Creating Your Wallet

Every user needs a digital wallet to store and send money. Your wallet is created once.

1. From the Dashboard, click **Create Wallet** (shown if you do not have one yet)
2. Your wallet is created instantly in XAF (Central African CFA franc)
3. Your unique **wallet number** is displayed (format: `WLT-YYYYMMDD-NNNNN`)

### Checking Your Balance

Your current balance is always visible on the **Dashboard** and the **Wallet** page.

### Depositing Funds

1. Go to **Wallet** in the navigation menu
2. Click **Deposit**
3. Enter the amount in XAF (minimum: 1 XAF, maximum: 10,000,000 XAF per transaction)
4. Add a description (e.g. "Salary deposit")
5. Click **Confirm Deposit**
6. Your balance updates immediately and you receive a confirmation email

### Withdrawing Funds

1. Go to **Wallet** in the navigation menu
2. Click **Withdraw**
3. Enter the amount (minimum: 1 XAF, maximum: 5,000,000 XAF per transaction)
4. Add a description
5. Click **Confirm Withdrawal**

> **Limits:** The maximum daily withdrawal total is 10,000,000 XAF. You cannot withdraw more than your current balance.

### Viewing Transaction History

Go to **Wallet → Transactions** to see a complete list of all deposits, withdrawals, and transfers on your wallet.

## Sending Money (P2P Transfer)

Send money to any other registered TT-BANK user instantly.

1. Go to **Transactions** in the navigation menu
2. Click **New Transfer**
3. Enter the **recipient's email address** (they must be a registered TT-BANK user with a wallet)
4. Enter the **amount** in XAF (minimum: 1 XAF, maximum: 5,000,000 XAF per transfer)
5. Add an optional **description** (e.g. "Rent payment")
6. Click **Send**
7. Review the summary and click **Confirm**

The money leaves your wallet and arrives in the recipient's wallet instantly. Both you and the recipient receive an email notification.

> **Cannot send to yourself.** The system prevents self-transfers.
> **Daily limit.** You can transfer a maximum of 10,000,000 XAF per day.

## Merchant Payments (QR Code)

Pay a registered merchant by scanning their QR code.

### For Customers

1. Ask the merchant to show their payment QR code (on their phone screen or printed)
2. Go to **Merchants → Pay** in the app
3. Enter or scan the **merchant code** from the QR code
4. Enter the **payment amount** in XAF
5. Enter your **wallet number** (shown on your Wallet page)
6. Click **Pay**
7. You receive a payment confirmation and email receipt

### For Merchants

**Registering as a merchant:**
1. Go to **Merchants → Register My Business**
2. Fill in:
   - Business name
   - Business email
   - Business phone
   - Business address
   - Business category (Retail, Food & Beverage, Healthcare, etc.)
   - Description of your business (optional)
   - Your TT-BANK wallet number (the wallet that will receive payments)
3. Click **Submit Registration**
4. Your registration is reviewed by an administrator. You will receive an email when your account is approved (status: **Active**).

**Generating your QR code:**
1. Once your account is **Active**, go to **Merchants → My Profile**
2. Click **View QR Code**
3. Download or print the QR code to display to customers

**Viewing your earnings:**
- Go to **Merchants → Dashboard** to see total revenue, number of payments, and today's activity
- Go to **Merchants → Payments** to see individual payment records

## Community Savings (Tontine / Njangi)

TT-BANK supports traditional rotating savings groups, known as *tontine* or *njangi* in Central Africa.

### How it works

A savings group has a fixed number of members, a fixed contribution amount, and a payout cycle (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly). Each cycle, every member contributes the fixed amount. The total pool is paid out to one member. The rotation continues until every member has received the payout exactly once.

**Example:** 5 members, 25,000 XAF contribution, monthly cycle.
- Each month, all 5 members contribute 25,000 XAF = 125,000 XAF pool
- Month 1: Member 1 receives 125,000 XAF
- Month 2: Member 2 receives 125,000 XAF
- ... and so on until all 5 members have received their payout

### Creating a Savings Group

1. Go to **Savings → Create Group**
2. Fill in:
   - **Group name** (3–100 characters)
   - **Description** (optional)
   - **Contribution amount** in XAF (minimum: 100 XAF)
   - **Payout cycle** — Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly
   - **Maximum members** — 2 to 50
   - **Start date** — must be a future date
   - **Your wallet number** — for your own contributions and payout
3. Click **Create Group**
4. You are automatically the first member (payout order: 1)
5. Share the group ID with people you want to invite

### Joining a Savings Group

1. Go to **Savings → Browse Groups**
2. Find the group you want to join (ask the creator for the group ID if needed)
3. Click **Join Group**
4. Enter **your wallet number** (where your contribution will be debited each cycle and your payout credited when it is your turn)
5. Click **Join**

> You cannot join a group that is already full or has already started.

### Making a Contribution

When it is time to contribute for the current cycle:

1. Go to **Savings → My Groups**
2. Select the group
3. Click **Contribute**
4. Enter **your wallet number**
5. Click **Confirm**

The fixed contribution amount is automatically debited from your wallet. You receive an email confirmation.

### Payout

When all members have contributed for the current cycle, the group creator (or any admin) can trigger the payout:

1. Go to the group page
2. Click **Process Payout**
3. The total pool is transferred to the next member's wallet
4. All members receive an email notification

## Account Security

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to your **Profile** (top-right menu)
2. Click **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password and your new password
4. Click **Save Changes**

### Logging Out

Click your name in the top-right corner and select **Logout**. Your session is immediately invalidated and cannot be reused even if someone has your token.

### Suspicious Activity

If you notice transactions you did not make, contact your administrator immediately. The audit log records every action with timestamp and IP address.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What currency does TT-BANK use?**
The primary currency is XAF (Central African CFA franc). Support for USD, EUR, and GBP is available.

**How long does a transfer take?**
Transfers between TT-BANK wallets are instant (typically under 1 second).

**What happens if a payment fails?**
If a payment or transfer fails (e.g. insufficient funds), no money leaves your wallet. You receive an email explaining the reason for the failure.

**Can I have more than one wallet?**
No. Each account has exactly one wallet.

**Is there a minimum balance I must keep?**
No. Your balance can be zero.

**What is the maximum group size for savings?**
A group can have between 2 and 50 members.

**I didn't receive my verification email. What should I do?**
Check your spam folder. If it is not there, wait 2 minutes and use the **Resend Code** option on the verification screen. Codes expire after 10 minutes.
